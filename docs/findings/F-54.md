# F-54 — Over the inline cap or past the IR depth bound, a call is a PLAIN `Call`, against ADR-0006's "every call … becomes its body"

- **Assumption**:
  - ADR-0006 "Inlining": "every call to a non-recursive function becomes the function's body, with
    closures substituted hygienically".
  - ADR-0040 "Inline cap" and F-44: past the cap or `MAX_IR_DEPTH` the filter is a Stub, "above it a
    Stub until 2b" (spec-2 grilling round 1, Q1).
- **How it fails**:
  - A call cannot always become its body. Inlining copies a body per call, and
    `def f1: .; def f2: f1|f1; …` doubles the copies with each definition, so the cap is soon passed.
  - A Stub there ends filters jq-ref runs. DEVIATIONS "IR depth" lists 513 and 30 001 levels of
    nested definitions and 500 nested value-parameter expansions.
  - A call that survived but charged nothing would escape the step budget:
    `def f1: .; def f2: f1|f1; …` would otherwise run 2^59 untimed calls; jq-ref runs the chain to
    `f22`, 2^21 calls, in 0.13 s, `null`.
- **Severity**: major (filters jq runs are Stubs).
- **Evidence**: spec 2b grilling round 1, Q2 (`docs/wayfinder/spec-2b-grilling.md`); DEVIATIONS "IR
  depth"; F-44.
- **Change made** (spec 2b):
  - Over the inline cap or past `MAX_IR_DEPTH`, a call is a PLAIN `Call` to a function lowered once
    with real closures (ADR-0045). It weighs stack units like RECURSIVE (ADR-0011) and counts toward
    the recursion-depth budget (ADR-0046).
  - A PLAIN call ticks one step.
  - Functions are lowered from a worklist, never inside their caller's lowering, so a function's
    lowering never stacks on its caller's.
  - The inline-or-call choice never expands a call twice, so compile work stays O(source + cap).
  - DEVIATIONS "IR depth": the three call rows go; the three chain rows (`.[.a:]`, `.[.a]`,
    alternating operators) stay Stubs.
  - ADR-0006 (inlining), ADR-0040 (inline cap, IR depth, cost), F-44's ruling, ADR-0008 (tick
    site), ADR-0011 (units) and DEVIATIONS "IR depth" are amended.
