# F-69 — `jn` and `yn` run loops that grow with the order, while a scalar builtin ticks nothing

- **Assumption**:
  - ADR-0034 (#65): "an operator on two scalars ticks nothing". Spec 4's draft, question 12:
    scalar math ticks nothing.
  - ADR-0008 and ADR-0017: the deadline and the interrupt flag are read only at a tick's refuel.
- **How it fails**:
  - `jn` and `yn` run a loop that grows with the order. A: `jn(2000000000; 1e9)` ran past a 20 s
    timeout on jq-ref; `yn(200000000; 1e9)` took 0.42 s.
  - A call that ticks nothing never reaches a refuel. An embedded run would outlive its 10 s
    deadline and ignore a thread interrupt.
- **Severity**: major (a filter could escape the deadline and cancellation).
- **Evidence**: spec 4 grilling round 1, M1 (`docs/wayfinder/spec-4-grilling.md`).
- **Change made** (spec 4, ADR-0051):
  - `jn` and `yn` tick once per 1 024 steps of any loop that grows with the order (ADR-0034's
    spec-4 tick table). Every other math builtin still ticks nothing.
  - The last math ticket's red has two witnesses. `jn(2000000000; 1e9)` under a short deadline
    ends with `JqTermination(BUDGET, DEADLINE)`; under a thread interrupt it ends with
    `CANCELLED`. The rate is pinned by two adjacent counts.
