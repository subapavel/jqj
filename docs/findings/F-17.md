# F-17: The ticket-gate measurements rest on a nearly empty suite

- **Assumption** (ADR-0014 ticket gate steps 1–2, ADR-0020 "JUnit and PIT" and "The compile-mode
  and backend oracles"): the skeleton ticket measures the oracle step against 60 s and the PIT step
  against 3 minutes, and only that measurement can move a step to the spec gate.
- **How it fails**: when the skeleton ticket runs, the suite holds a few cases; the oracle and PIT
  costs grow with every spec (at least 28 s per surviving mutant at 5000 cases, ADR-0020). A step
  that passes at the skeleton can exceed its target in spec 3 with no rule to move it.
- **Severity**: minor.
- **Evidence**: issue #17 round 1 (the record of the designer's draft and the adviser's
  evaluation, posted on #17).
- **Change made**: ADR-0023: at each spec gate the verifier reports the slowest ticket-gate steps
  of the spec's tickets; one above its target applies the fallback ADR-0014 and ADR-0020 already
  name, by a finding, with no new owner round (owner). ADR-0014 and ADR-0020 amended.
