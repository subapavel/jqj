# F-84 — The spec gate's PIT step runs two slow test classes at every minion start

- **Assumption**:
  - ADR-0014, spec gate step 2: "PIT on whole class-C modules (with oracle's crossModule run, #45).
    Target (F-28): under 45 min alone on `claude-cloud-4vcpu`; above that, ADR-0020's seed fallback by
    a finding."
  - ADR-0023: "for PIT, seeds are grouped first".
- **How it fails**:
  - Spec 2a's gate (#56) ran `pit` for 14 662 s (4 h 04 min) and was stopped by hand (rc 143,
    `EXECUTED_FAIL`). query-core finished in about 4 min (308 mutants, 293 killed). jq-core had done 46
    of 94 units and 3 259 mutants when it was stopped. oracle and jq-cli were `NOT_RUN`.
  - Cause, from `javap` on pitest 1.30.2 and pitest-junit5-plugin 1.2.3: every minion start finds its
    tests through `Launcher.execute`, so it runs its test classes once before any mutant. Two classes
    carry most of that cost: `WideConstructStepsTest` (116.4 s) and `OperatorChainTest` (35.7 s), of
    197 s for all of jq-core's classes. They are the first killer of only 8 and 4 of the 3 259
    mutants.
  - Every TIMED_OUT or MEMORY_ERROR restarts a minion and pays that cost again. The run had 53 and 6.
  - The oracle run puts jq-core's and query-core's test classes on its classpath (`oracle/pom.xml`,
    `additionalClasspathElements`), so the cost is paid twice.
  - The seed fallback speeds up oracle's cases, not jq-core's own tests, so it does not apply.
- **Severity**: blocking (spec 2a cannot pass its gate).
- **Evidence** (ticket #190):
  - `.logs/spec56-pit-stopped.log`: lines 326 and 366 give the class times.
  - `.logs/spec56-pit-jqcore-partial-mutations.xml`, the partial report.
  - `.logs/spec56-steps.tsv`: `pit 14661849 143`.
  - The grilling-adviser's ruling of 2026-11-14.
- **Change made** (proposed by the grilling-adviser for the owner under the delegation of 2026-11-14):
  - (a) Both classes go into `excludedTestClasses`, in `jq-core/pom.xml` and in oracle's list. It must
    never be done as a `-D` property.
    - Each line's comment gives the class's seconds and its first-kill count.
    - Both classes still run in step 1 of both gates. Leaving a test out of PIT's set can only lower the
      score, so this does not weaken a test under CLAUDE.md.
    - `quality/floors.tsv` and `ratchet/` are not touched. A mutant that now survives gets a fast test,
      or the gate stays red. Any further exclusion needs a finding.
  - (b) If oracle's tests do not use jq-core's or query-core's test classes at run time, oracle's two
    `additionalClasspathElements` go. `ratchet.java` already unions each module's own kills with the
    crossModule kills, which keeps "every test in the reactor". This amends ADR-0014's
    `additionalClasspathElements` sentence.
  - (c) Only if (a) and (b) still measure over 45 min: `threads` 2 in the root POM.
    - Going to 3 needs a measured peak RSS. 4 is never allowed.
    - TIMED_OUT and MEMORY_ERROR counts are reported before and after. The baseline is 53 and 6, and a
      rise is a finding.
    - Raising `timeoutConstant` is allowed. Lowering a timeout or a heap for speed is not.
  - Rejected:
    - `withHistory`: verdicts from earlier runs would enter the ratchet.
    - A jq-core subset: it breaks "whole class-C modules".
    - `mutationUnitSize`: it means more minion starts.
    - A smaller heap or timeout: it gives false kills.
  - The 45-min target stays, because it protects the nightly's 60-min timeout (F-28).
  - The verifier measures each of (a), (b) and (c) alone and records the results in
    `bench/RESULTS.md`: `Completed in` per module, the two counts and the score per module.
  - ADR-0014 step 2 and ADR-0020 "JUnit and PIT" are amended by this finding.
- **Open**:
  - The own-run scores are 95 % for query-core and about 93.6 % for jq-core (partial). The ratchet is
    at 100 % and 98 % (`ratchet/mutation.tsv`). Whether oracle's kills close that gap only the rerun
    shows.
  - The gate printed "slowest step 1: no timing in .logs/", so F-28's 120 s step-1 target went
    unreported. That is for a later round.
- **Measured at #190** (implementer, jq-core alone with `-pl query-core,jq-core -am`; logs in the
  ticket worktree's `.logs/190-*`):

  | Run | query-core | jq-core | jq-core TIMED_OUT / MEMORY_ERROR | jq-core score |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | (a)+(b), 1 thread | 240 s | 3 380 s | 54 / 7 | 3 213 / 3 439 (93 %) |
  | (c), 2 threads | 152 s | 2 008 s | 57 / 4 | 3 213 / 3 439 (93 %) |

  - With 2 threads, TIMED_OUT rises by 3 of 3 439 mutants and the kills are unchanged. The architect
    records that rise here, as this finding's own rule asks, and adds no new finding.
  - Peak RSS with 2 threads: 8.3 GiB of 16 GiB.
  - The 12 mutants that only the two excluded classes killed first now survive jq-core's own run:
    4 in `Resolver`, 7 in `Binder` and 1 in `ArrayView.get`. The spec gate's oracle run decides them.
