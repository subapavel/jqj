# F-32 — Two more tick sites: a comma's right branch and each further `?//` alternative

- **Assumption** (ADR-0008 "Budgets"): steps are charged only at a loop back-edge, a tail jump, a
  `RECURSIVE` or real-closure call, and in native loops, so a filter cannot run long without a tick.
- **How it fails**: a chain of commas multiplies results with no loop. jq-ref runs
  `(1,2)|…|empty` with 22 commas in 0.42 s; with 40 commas jqj would never tick, so the step
  budget, the deadline and cancellation would all be blind. A chain of `?//` grows the same way
  (16 → 18 alternatives: 26 → 139 ms in jq-ref).
- **Severity**: major (a budget hole).
- **Evidence**: spec 2 grilling round 2, Q4 (owner yes, 2026-11-12); jq-ref timings by the
  grilling-adviser.
- **Change made**: two more IR-defined tick sites: entering `Comma`'s right branch, and entering
  each `AltBind` alternative after the first. Ticks are still not charged per node or per result, so
  G1 and G2 pay nothing new, and both backends still stop at the same step. ADR-0008 is amended to
  point here.
