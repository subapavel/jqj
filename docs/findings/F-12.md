# F-12: A document is a `byte[]`, so at most 2^31−3 bytes, and native input is copied once

- **Assumption** (ADR-0002, founding §6.1 and §11): "4 GiB per document"; "zero-copy input: a
  `MemorySegment` (heap `byte[]`, direct buffer, mapped file)"; in Rail8 (R8) "a spooled body is
  mapped in an `Arena` for the step".
- **How it fails**: the only measured reader loop runs over `byte[]` (`m1NoDup`, "Tape reader for milestone 1" in `bench/RESULTS.md`, 34.3 ms per 10 MB),
  and joni takes `byte[]` only. A `byte[]` holds at most 2^31−3 bytes on JDK 25.0.5
  (`new byte[Integer.MAX_VALUE-2]` passes the VM's size check, `MAX_VALUE-1` fails). A native,
  mapped or read-only segment has no backing array (`heapBase()` is empty) and must be copied.
  The owner confirmed (2026-11-11) that R8 never hands a whole body as a direct or mapped
  buffer: a whole-document step copies the body once into a `byte[]`, only below the blob store's
  tree limit (always under 2 GiB), and larger bodies are only streamed in chunks.
- **Severity**: minor.
- **Evidence**: issue #11 round 1 (adviser: `javap` and a probe of `MemorySegment.heapBase()`;
  owner's answer on R8 bodies).
- **Change made**: ADR-0015 reads a writable heap segment in place and copies any other once.
  ADR-0002's document limit becomes 2^31−3 bytes (amendment), and so does the line in
  `DEVIATIONS.md`. Founding §6.1 and the §11 R8 sentence are read through ADR-0015: zero copy
  holds for heap input; a mapped spooled body is not a milestone-1 input. A chunked reader is a later
  item.
