# F-31 — ADR-0006's "only natives" sentence names jq-defined builtins only

- **Assumption** (ADR-0006 "Builtins"): "the only natives are `range/2`, `inputs`, `last/1` and
  `flatten`".
- **How it fails**: read literally, the sentence forbids natives jq itself has in C. In jq 1.8.2,
  `builtin.c` bytecodes `empty`, `not`, `path/1`, `last/1` and `range/2`, and `length`, `type`,
  `has`, `keys_unsorted`, `tostring`, `getpath`, `setpath`, `delpaths`, `error`, `sort`, `input`
  and the rest of `function_list` are C functions with no jq definition. Spec 3's scope already
  names such natives.
- **Severity**: minor (wording).
- **Evidence**: jq 1.8.2 `src/builtin.c` (bytecoded builtins and `function_list`), read by the
  grilling-adviser for spec 2 round 1 (2026-11-12).
- **Change made**: the sentence means jq's *jq-defined* builtins: of those, only `inputs` and
  `flatten` become natives, and `to_entries` since ADR-0044 (#85) (beside the bytecoded `range/2` and `last/1`); every builtin jq writes in
  C or bytecode is a native in jqj. ADR-0006 is amended to point here.
