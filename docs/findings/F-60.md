# F-60 — `=` is not `|=` on an atomic right side

- **Assumption**:
  - ADR-0005 "Nodes": "Paths: `PathOf`, `Update` (`|=`)"; "Assignments `=`, `op=` and `//=` lower
    to a `Bind` of the right side around an `Update`".
  - ADR-0010 "Updates in every shape": the first right-hand result wins, and an empty right-hand
    side deletes the path.
- **How it fails**: on jq-ref the two limits differ by one (n is the path length; getpath forms
  and literal `.a` chains of 9 999, 10 000 and 10 001 agree):
  - `|=`, `op=` and `//=` raise "Path too deep" at n ≥ 10 001 before the right side runs, and at
    n = 10 000 after a right-side result: at 10 000, `|= (debug|error("rhs"))` printed the debug
    line and raised "rhs"; at 10 001, `|= (debug|1)` printed nothing and raised "Path too deep"
    (corrected at spec 3's ticket approval, 2026-11-14).
  - `=` raises only at n ≥ 10 001, as `|= empty`, `del`, `getpath`, `setpath` and `delpaths` do.
  - So an `=` lowered to a `|=` Update raises at n = 10 000 where jq-ref gives a value. It would
    also read the old leaf, which `=` never needs.
- **Severity**: minor (one boundary; an error where jq answers).
- **Evidence**: spec 3 grilling round 1, questions 2 and 8.
- **Change made**:
  - `Update.Kind {MODIFY, ASSIGN}` (ADR-0048). ASSIGN takes an atomic right side, never reads the
    old leaf, never deletes, and its limit is one higher.
  - ADR-0005's node list and assignment bullet, and ADR-0029 (the limits), are amended.
