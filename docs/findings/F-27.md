# F-27 — The ticket gate misses both its time targets; PIT leaves it

- **Assumption** (ADR-0014 ticket gate steps 1 and 2; ADR-0020 "JUnit and PIT"; ADR-0023 "The
  fallbacks fire at any spec gate"; F-19): with three gates in parallel, step 1 (`verify`) is under
  60 s and step 2 (PIT on the changed classes) is under 3 minutes. If not, the compile-mode oracle
  moves to the spec gate (step 1); for PIT, generated seeds are first grouped or kept out of PIT,
  and only if that is not enough does PIT leave the ticket gate.
- **How it fails**: #40 measured both steps on `claude-cloud-4vcpu` at `eee8bb3` (ref `7e7aac5`,
  8 changed source files, 642 mutants in jq-core, 661 in oracle):
  - step 1, three in parallel: 70.2 to 71.4 s (alone 27.4 to 30.1 s). The oracle tests take
    about 2.7 s; the rest is the reactor build. No compile-mode oracle exists yet, so its fallback
    has nothing to move.
  - step 2, three in parallel: 1052.9 to 1077.1 s; alone 728.0 to 741.8 s. With the 472 passing
    `gen/values-v1` seeds removed it is still 532.0 to 537.4 s alone, about 3 times the target.
    Keeping seeds out of PIT is not enough.
- **Severity**: major (it binds every later ticket's gate and the writers' wait).
- **Evidence**: #40, `bench/RESULTS.md` section "Ticket-gate steps (#40)", commit `9bfb797`.
- **Change made**:
  - PIT leaves the ticket gate. `scripts/gate-ticket.sh` loses step 2 (`pit`, `pit-floor`).
    PIT runs only in the spec gate (whole class-C modules, against the floor). This is ADR-0014's
    named fallback, applied with no new owner round (ADR-0023, F-17). Ticket #44 makes the
    script change.
  - The compile-mode oracle, when a later spec builds it, runs in the spec gate, not the ticket
    gate (ADR-0020's named fallback for step 1).
  - Step 1 stays above 60 s with three gates in parallel, because of the reactor build. No ADR
    names a further fallback, so it is reported to the owner in the spec PR, not changed here.
  - ADR-0014, ADR-0020 and ADR-0023 are amended to point here. At later spec gates the verifier
    reports the slowest ticket-gate step 1 only.
- **Amended by F-46** (#89): the compile-mode oracle runs in the ticket gate after all. Step 1 is
  `./mvnw -B -Pbench,compile-oracle verify`, so `ratchet.java check` checks `compile.txt` there and
  no longer prints `NOT_RUN compile`. In #89's gate run the only `NOT_RUN` lines left were the PIT
  reports (`mutation jq-core`, `mutation query-core`): PIT still runs only in the spec gate. Added
  time, one gate alone on this cloud container (4 vCPU), two runs each: the two compile-mode test
  classes take 5.3 to 7.1 s (3 575 and 3 189 tests); the oracle module takes 17.8
  to 19.9 s instead of 12.2 to 13.2 s; step 1 takes 61.6 to 64.7 s instead of 57.6 to 62.1 s.
